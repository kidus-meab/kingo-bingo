Kingo Bingo Telegram Mini App

kingo bingo is a telegram mini app that is launched as mini app in the telegram application 
and let people play bingo.

Bingo Game Rules
Players join a game room and each player receives a randomly generated Bingo card.
Each card has a 5×5 grid with columns B, I, N, G, O.
Numbers are arranged by column:
B: 1–15
I: 16–30
N: 31–45
G: 46–60
O: 61–75
The center square is usually a FREE space.
The game randomly draws one number at a time.
If the called number exists on a player's card, it is automatically marked.
Players win when they complete the required pattern.
Common winning patterns:
Horizontal row
Vertical column
Diagonal
Four corners
Full card / blackout
When a player completes a winning pattern, they can press BINGO.
The system verifies the card automatically.
If the Bingo is valid, the player is announced as the winner and the round ends.
If multiple players complete the winning pattern on the same number, they can be co-winners.
Numbers already called cannot be called again.
A new round generates new cards and resets the called numbers.
